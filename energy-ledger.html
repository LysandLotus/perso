How this is worked out. Resting burn uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for your profile (172 cm, 32 yr) × a light-daily-activity factor. Ride calories use standard MET values for stationary cycling at each intensity, counted on top of resting burn so nothing is double-added. Weekly fat loss assumes ~7,700 kcal per kg. These are solid estimates, not lab readings — treat them as a guide and trust the multi-week trend on your scale and waist tape over any single number.
